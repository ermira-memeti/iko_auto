# IKO-Auto — Reviewer 1 Comment 3: graph-coverage audit

This is a **post-hoc, non-held-out** question-level diagnostic of frozen G0/G+ source-linked assertion coverage against 50 Development and 50 H1 questions. No original retrieval result or historical relevance judgment was changed.

## Files / locations

- `labels/graph_coverage/COMMENT3_SUBMITTED_100_SUPPORT_LABELS.csv`: de-identified support grades as present in two matching submitted assessment sheets, preserving three source-grounding-unclear records.
- `results/graph_coverage/COMMENT3_GRAPH_SUPPORT_COUNTS.csv`: manuscript Table 14 counts.
- `results/graph_coverage/COMMENT3_FIVE_DIRECT_RETRIEVAL.csv`: exploratory 5-question Development-only retrieval metrics using M2/M5/M7/M8 original archived rankings and passage labels.
- `results/graph_coverage/COMMENT3_AUTHOR_SOURCE_CHECKS.csv`: author-side validation of original source-PDF pages for five directly supported G+ questions, plus matching policy clauses for three questions that assessors marked source-grounding-unclear.
- `reproduce/verify_comment3_coverage.py`: standard-library verification of counts and five-question retrieval scores against existing `results/development/DEV_RANKINGS_PUBLIC.csv` and `labels/development_adjudicated_519_public.csv`.

## Scientific qualification

The two submitted coding sheets record identical categorical judgments but that does not independently establish their accuracy or the assessors' independence. The assessors did not supply PDF-page citations for the five direct cases; the pages in the author audit are **author-checked** and are **not attributed to the assessors**. Original unclear statuses are retained, even though author searches found corresponding clauses. Five G+ direct questions all belong to Development, the set which informed graph extension. No H1 question was classified directly supported by the graph. Comparisons on the five Development questions are descriptive and not proof of general graph-retrieval benefit.

Graph citation to the correct PDF page is not sufficient to prove full policy interpretation or legal entailment. The study has no independent exhaustive inventory of all clauses across the five booklets. Third-party insurance PDFs, private assessor forms and AI study drafts are **not included**.

The data and text should not be described as a new independent held-out experiment or as an evaluation of complete ontology clause coverage.
