# Third-party source data

The motor-insurance policy PDFs used in the experiments are third-party source documents and are not redistributed here.

The registered filenames and SHA-256 hashes are available in `reproduce/h1/H1_MANIFEST.json` and `results/h1/H1_RUN_PROVENANCE.json`. Users who wish to perform a fresh PDF-to-ranking execution must obtain authorized copies of the same document editions and place them under `data/research_corpus/`.

Public ranking, relevance and audit derivatives minimize reproduced policy text and identify passages primarily by document, page and chunk ID. The frozen RDF graphs retain selected short quotations because those quotations are part of the provenance representation evaluated in the paper.
